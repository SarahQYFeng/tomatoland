Sarah is a graphic designer & game developer based in Providence and Beijing whose work spans editorial, branding, posters, UIUX, and exhibition design. Sarah always work with the translation between physical and digital, and believe that communication is the core of design. She has worked with volume 1 and United Design Lab on editorial and brand-related projects.

Sarah is graduating from Rhode Island School of Design, and is jealous of everyone on earth who has seen a volcanic eruption. She puts this in her bio forever until she actually sees one.
